OUR COMPANY

About Hauslysmart

Smart Solutions for Everyday Living

Hauslysmart is a sub-brand of Kanway, supported by its research, manufacturing and service capabilities. Kanway traces its origins to 1996, with Guangzhou Kanway Electrical Appliances Co., Ltd. as its predecessor. Its core products include intelligent electronic switches, thermostats, hotel door signs and key-card power switches.

We believe technology should make life simpler and more comfortable. Our engineering, manufacturing and quality teams work together to support partners from the initial concept through to finished products.
