PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

Six Stages, Connected by Review Points

The actual route and checks depend on the selected model and agreed requirements.

  1. 01Material Preparation
  2. 02PCB & SMT Production
  3. 03Product Assembly
  4. 04Configuration & Functional Review
  5. 05Final Inspection
  6. 06Packaging & Dispatch Preparation

STAGE 01

Material Preparation

Match components and information to the confirmed product version.

What we review

  • Component and version references
  • Incoming condition and inspection requirements
  • Materials prepared for the next stage

Review methods and acceptance criteria are confirmed for the product and project.

STAGE 02

PCB & SMT Production

Produce the electronic module through controlled placement and soldering.

What we review

  • Component placement and soldering
  • Process checks relevant to the assembly
  • Electronic module prepared for assembly

Review methods and acceptance criteria are confirmed for the product and project.

STAGE 03

Product Assembly

Bring electronic modules, housings and mechanical parts together.

What we review

  • Module and housing fit
  • Electrical connections and fastening
  • Assembly condition and appearance

Review methods and acceptance criteria are confirmed for the product and project.

STAGE 04

Configuration & Functional Review

Configure the selected version and check the relevant product functions.

What we review

  • Firmware and configuration references
  • Functions and interfaces for the model
  • Findings requiring review or rework

Review methods and acceptance criteria are confirmed for the product and project.

STAGE 05

Final Inspection

Review the completed product against the agreed inspection scope.

What we review

  • Appearance and product identification
  • Applicable functional checks
  • Inspection status and outstanding issues

Review methods and acceptance criteria are confirmed for the product and project.

STAGE 06

Packaging & Dispatch Preparation

Prepare the product, accessories and delivery information.

What we review

  • Packaging and label configuration
  • Accessories and product documents
  • Shipment handover information

Review methods and acceptance criteria are confirmed for the product and project.

BEFORE PRODUCTION

Confirm the Production Version

A clear product version keeps the order, sample and production information aligned.

InformationWhat to confirm
ProductModel, version and configuration
MarketInstallation environment and destination market
BrandingColour, marking, logo and packaging
Quantity & timingOrder volume, sample needs and expected schedule
Review scopeInspection items, required documents and agreed criteria
