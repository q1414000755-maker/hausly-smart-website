PRODUCT & PROJECT INQUIRY CHECKLIST
Hauslysmart | Enquiry preparation guide | 2026-10-13

1. YOUR BUSINESS
Company name, contact person, business email, destination country and your business role.

2. PRODUCT SELECTION
Product names or model references, preferred finish and layout, requested quantity per model, and whether you need samples or production quantities.

3. PROJECT REQUIREMENTS
Application, installation or wiring conditions, preferred connectivity, required product documents and any target market requirements. Include project drawings or reference materials when available.

4. CUSTOMIZATION
Logo, packaging, labeling, documentation and other requirements. State which details are fixed and which can be discussed.

5. DELIVERY PLANNING
Delivery destination, requested timing, preferred shipping arrangement and any packaging or shipping mark requirements.

6. ITEMS TO CONFIRM IN THE QUOTATION
Final product specification, sample availability and cost, minimum order quantities, pricing, payment terms, preparation time, delivery arrangement and applicable after-sales terms.

Product-specific details and commercial arrangements require confirmation for your selected models and order. This checklist does not confirm an order or establish pricing, delivery dates or warranty terms.
