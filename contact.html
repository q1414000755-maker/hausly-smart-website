    FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

    A Few Useful Answers.

    Helpful information for your enquiry.

    What should I include when requesting a quotation?+

    Share the product model or category, required configuration, estimated quantity and destination country. Mention any branding, packaging or delivery requirements. Final pricing and commercial terms are confirmed for the agreed configuration.

    Can I request samples before a bulk order?+

    Choose Product enquiry and tell us which model you would like to evaluate. Include your installation requirements and delivery destination so sample availability and terms can be discussed.

    What information helps with an OEM / ODM project?+

    Describe the target market, product concept, branding or technical changes, estimated volume and desired schedule. A short brief is enough to start; scope and feasibility are reviewed with the project requirements.

    How do I get help with an existing product or order?+

    Choose Technical support and include the model, order reference if available, installation conditions and a clear description of the issue. The team can then identify the relevant information or next steps.

    How can I arrange a factory visit?+

    Choose Factory visit and share preferred dates, visitor count and areas of interest. Confirm the visit schedule and location with the team before making travel arrangements.

    How is the lead time confirmed?+

    Share the product model, configuration, estimated quantity and any customization requirements. Production timing and delivery arrangements can then be reviewed for your enquiry and confirmed with the quotation or order details.
